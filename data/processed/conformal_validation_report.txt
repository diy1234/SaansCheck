SAANSCHECK CHRONOLOGICAL SPLIT-CONFORMAL INTERVAL VALIDATION
====================================================================

DECISION
Intervals are NOT integrated into the Historical AQI Forecast dashboard.
The saved dashboard models were trained through 2022, so 2021-2022
cannot be used as independent calibration targets for those same fitted
production models. The experiment below evaluates a separate in-memory
Random Forest trained only through 2020; its interval radii do not apply
to the saved production model forecasts.

METHOD AND DATA BOUNDARIES
Method: split conformal prediction with absolute residual scores; the
finite-sample one-indexed quantile rank is ceil((n + 1) * 0.90).
Each direct horizon uses a separate Random Forest. Calibration is done
separately by station and horizon to avoid pooling station error scales.
Predictors use the existing 22-column training feature contract only.
Targets are grouped by location and partitioned by their actual target
dates; no future target is used as a predictor.
Training target dates: through 2020-12-31.
Calibration target dates: 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31.
Evaluation target dates: 2022-01-01 through 2023-12-31.
No model or joblib artifact was saved or modified.

INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
The experiment reports observed empirical coverage and average interval
width only. Ordinary split-conformal finite-sample coverage relies on
exchangeability; daily AQI is serially dependent and seasonal, so the
nominal 90% does not guarantee 90% coverage on this or every future
distribution, especially under temporal or geographic distribution shift.
The evaluation is independent of this experiment's model fitting and
calibration by date, but is not an independent assessment of the saved
production-model intervals. Existing walk-forward and 2023 model-evaluation
artifacts were read only and were not rewritten.

The CSV includes pooled and per-station metrics, station/horizon-specific
calibration sample counts, evaluation sample counts, quantile radii, and
the exact target-date boundaries.
